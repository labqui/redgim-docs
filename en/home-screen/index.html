The home screen

The first time you open REDGIM, a quick tour points out the main buttons. It shows once per installed version: after an app update it comes back, to show what changed.

There are six ways in. Two start a new analysis, one takes you to what already exists, one builds the analysis from scratch, and two talk about the app itself.

Prediction Mode

To estimate a value from colour, typically a concentration. It needs samples of known value, the standards, which teach the relationship between colour and amount. How it works

Comparative Mode

For when the question is not "how much" but which ones look alike. It needs no known values. How it works

Open an analysis

The list of what you have already analysed. From there you can review results, add images, redo the calculation with other settings or export. My analyses

Start an analysis

The full wizard, which walks the setup stage by stage and leaves no setting implicit. The step by step

About REDGIM

Who made it, which version you are on, the terms of use and the publications tied to the app.

It opens three tabs:

TabWhat it holds
Citationspublished work that used or cited REDGIM. The list is fetched from a public index, so it grows without updating the app
Authorswho develops and maintains the project
Aboutthe installed version, the person responsible and the software registration

The version shown in that last tab is the one to quote when citing the app or reporting a problem.

Help, the ? in the top right corner

Asks whether you want to open the manual and, on confirmation, opens this page in the browser. It is not what replays the tour.

Which of the first four?

If you already know whether you want to predict or to compare, go straight in through the first two: they skip the questions your choice has already answered. The full wizard is there for when you want to see every option before deciding.
