Automatic cuvette detection

Marking regions of interest one by one with your fingers can be time-consuming and prone to small framing inconsistencies. To streamline routine laboratory workflows, REDGIM features embedded artificial intelligence that recognizes colorimetric and spectrophotometric cuvettes in real time directly through the camera.

When pointing at a cuvette rack, the app identifies each cuvette, determines where the liquid sample is most homogeneous, and automatically places the region of interest (ROI) when the photo is taken.

Everything runs on-device, with no internet connection required, using either the device camera or an external USB webcam connected via OTG cable.

Enabling cuvette detection​

In the upper controls of the camera screen, tap the magic wand button:

  1. The AI settings dialog will appear.
  2. Toggle on Detect cuvettes.
  3. The magic wand icon will display a green badge, confirming that real-time computer vision is active.
  4. Adjust the Minimum confidence slider if needed (the recommended default is 80%).

What appears on screen​

As soon as the camera frames the rack or cuvettes, visual markers appear over the preview:

  • Cyan bounding box: outlines the detected outer boundaries of each cuvette.
  • Confidence percentage: shows the computer vision confidence score (e.g., 88%).
  • Dashed white rectangle: indicates exactly where the ROI will be created if the photo is captured at that moment.

The bounding boxes feature intelligent temporal stabilization: slight hand tremors or momentary score fluctuations will not cause flickering or jitter.

Where is the ROI positioned?​

The algorithm does not place the ROI arbitrarily. It follows strict geometric rules designed specifically for spectrophotometry and colorimetry:

1. Focused on the liquid, clear of the meniscus​

For upright cuvettes, the upper portion contains the curved liquid meniscus, which causes strong optical reflections. The automatic ROI is centered at 25% distance from the cuvette base, where the liquid column is deep, clear, and free from surface reflections.

2. Safety margin away from sidewalls​

To prevent the sampling area from touching the glass, plastic, or quartz sidewalls (where refraction distorts color), the ROI dimensions are conservatively proportioned: 20% of the cuvette width and 10% of its height.

3. Horizontal cuvettes​

If a cuvette is positioned horizontally, the ROI is centered at the midpoint of the container.

Behavior by capture mode​

In multiple capture (multiple cuvettes in one photo)​

When capturing a rack holding standards and samples:

  • REDGIM generates one ROI for each detected cuvette.
  • ROIs are indexed in natural reading order: row by row, from left to right, matching standard laboratory rack preparation.
  • Unified size: the app computes the bounding boxes of all cuvettes and applies the dimensions of the smallest detected cuvette across all ROIs. This ensures every ROI remains fully inscribed inside its cuvette and guarantees chemometric measurement comparability.

In single capture (one photo per sample)​

When using single capture mode:

  • The system evaluates all detections and automatically selects the cuvette closest to the center of the crosshair, ignoring adjacent containers at the edges of the frame.

Minimum confidence adjustment​

Inside the magic wand settings menu, the slider allows fine-tuning detector sensitivity:

SensitivityConfidence RangeWhen to use
Default (Recommended)80%Ideal for most well-illuminated benches and clear cuvettes.
Strict85% to 95%For cluttered backgrounds with objects that could trigger false positives (test tubes, pens, grids).
Permissive60% to 75%For dark cuvettes, turbid/opaque solutions, or low-light conditions.

Bench best practices​

To ensure optimal automatic detection:

Tips for instant detection
  1. Uniform, contrasting background: place a white sheet or neutral background behind the cuvette rack.
  2. Diffuse lighting: avoid harsh point-source lights shining directly at the cuvettes to minimize specular reflections.
  3. Keep the rack aligned: face the cuvettes straight on, avoiding steep top-down or oblique angles.

Once captured, all ROIs appear immediately on the editor screen, ready for concentration assignments or classification labels.
